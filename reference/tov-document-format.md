## **5. Hard Rules**

Non-negotiable. Any copy that breaks these gets rewritten.

* **No em-dashes.** Never. Use commas, full stops, or restructure the sentence.
* **No hype.** No "game-changing", "revolutionary", "incredible results", "crushing it".
* **No vendor framing.** Never position yourself as a supplier selling time. You are a peer.
* **No unrealistic promises.** Never guarantee outcomes.
* **Under 90 words per email.** Ideally under 80. Never exceed 90.
* **British English throughout.** Spelling, phrasing, tone.
* **Hedge language for assumptions.** Use "guessing", "curious if", "typically", "I imagine".
* **No emojis.** None. Ever. In any outreach copy.
* **No bolding or italics in emails.** Clean plain text only.
* **Short lines, white space.** Scannable. Not walls of text.
* **3rd-5th grade reading level.** Simple words. Short sentences. Clear meaning.
